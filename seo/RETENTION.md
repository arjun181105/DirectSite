# Enquiry retention

Approved by the owner on 12 September 2026: retain enquiry records for six calendar months from receipt. The existing email address and legal trading name are unchanged. No terms page is requested.

Enquiries travel through Web3Forms to the existing recipient inbox. The static website does not have an enquiry database. Six months is the business retention policy; automatic deletion in Web3Forms and the recipient mailbox has not been configured or verified.

Operational implementation: identify DirectSite enquiry messages by their form sender and subject, review records older than six calendar months, and delete enquiry records that have reached their retention date from each provider and any exports. Preserve unrelated mail and records required for an ongoing customer engagement or an applicable recordkeeping obligation. Provider backup deletion remains subject to the provider's own policy. Do not use a whole-inbox deletion rule.

Keep an internal record of completion. Before promising automatic deletion publicly, verify the available Web3Forms retention setting and the mailbox deletion mechanism. Analytics retention is separate from enquiry retention.
